# Signal CRM — Terms of Service (draft for §20.10)

> **Status: draft.** Plain-language, not legal advice. Review before the hosted
> instance accepts its first real user.

## 1. What this is

Signal is a CRM for solo freelance developers: client relationships, projects,
GitHub activity, email/WhatsApp messaging, invoicing, and payments. It's a
tool for **your** freelance practice — one operator per account.

## 2. Your account

- You're responsible for the security of your login and for activity under your
  account.
- API keys are scoped to your account. Treat them like passwords: the raw key is
  shown once and never again. Revoke keys you no longer use.
- You can close your account at any time (see the Privacy Policy for what
  deletion does — including the export-first prompt for active invoices and the
  audit-log retention exception).

## 3. Your data and your clients' data

- You're responsible for making sure you have the right to store and process the
  client data you put into Signal, and that your client communication complies
  with applicable law.
- **Money fields are integers in minor units** — the product never does
  float math on currency.
- Invoices are financial records. Destroying an issued invoice is a worse
  outcome than a manual export, which is why account deletion refuses to delete
  active invoices without your explicit export-first confirmation.

## 4. Third-party services

Signal integrates with the services listed in the Privacy Policy (GitHub,
Google/Gmail, Meta/WhatsApp, Stripe/Paystack, Resend/Brevo, Mailgun/Postmark,
Groq/Gemini, Cloudflare R2). Your use of those services is subject to their own
terms. Signal's free-tier architecture means those services may change their
free-tier terms; we don't control that.

## 5. Acceptable use

Don't use Signal to: store or transmit unlawful content; harass or spam anyone
(including through the forwarding/WhatsApp integrations — note that
business-initiated WhatsApp messages outside the 24-hour window may incur Meta
fees); attempt to access another account's data; or abuse the API (rate limits
apply).

## 6. Liability

Signal is provided "as is," without warranty of any kind. To the maximum extent
permitted by law, the operator is not liable for indirect, incidental, or
consequential damages — including financial loss from a payment/invoice
discrepancy or lost data. You're running a business with this tool; verify the
important numbers.

## 7. Termination

You can stop using Signal at any time and delete your account. We can suspend or
terminate access for abuse of the service (spam, attempted unauthorized access,
violating this agreement), with notice where practical.

## 8. Changes

We'll update these terms as the product changes and note the date of the last
change.
